# Prayer area sprite prompts

Each sprite used a separate built-in ImageGen request with a transparent background. The exported RGBA sprites preserve the source alpha with a centered 88 × 88 pixel drawing on a 96 × 96 pixel canvas. The game and the Storybook preview draw the images at 0.9 opacity.

## Shared prompt

```text
Create a single original transparent RGBA PNG game effect asset for a top-down medieval pixel-art RPG. It is a floor decal that marks the range of a prayer centered at the player's feet, installed at 96×96 world pixels. Use a square canvas, an exactly centered truly circular silhouette in direct overhead orthographic view, centered at 50%/50%, with symmetrical bounds and 8% empty padding all around. A simple readable narrow perimeter ring, made from a few chunky intentional pixel clusters, holds a distinctive repeating ornament tucked entirely inside the circle. Keep middle 55% genuinely alpha-transparent and most of the circle interior open; ground and small characters will be visible over this layer. Restrained pixel art, sharp un-antialiased 16-bit style; a slim dark tone plus a light highlight for readings on grass and stone. Strong visual silhouette in thumbnail. Outer background is fully transparent alpha, no text, letters, backdrop, checkerboard, soft glow, gradients, solid fill, shadows, scene, perspective, or angle. One centered sprite only.
```

## Individual prompt endings

- `hearth.png` — Hearth prayer: a warm amber and ochre circle ornamented with small angular tongues of fire facing inward at intervals, dark sienna and bright honey edge accents.
- `road.png` — Traveler prayer: a mint and foliage-green circle with four sweeping paired wind arcs flowing around the ring, dark pine and pale spring-green edge accents.
- `lantern.png` — Clarity prayer: a blue and icy cyan circle with small four-point stars and thin angular constellation thread joining stars along the ring, dark ink blue and bright icy highlights. Leave the center clear.
- `grave.png` — Ash prayer: a dusty violet circular rim made of broken angular stone blocks and small clefts, deep plum and pale lavender edge highlights, readable broken pattern. All chips stay inside the circular footprint.
- `woven.png` — Woven prayer: a round two-strand braid of interlaced gold and red copper ribbons, visible over-and-under crossings near the perimeter, deep burgundy and bright pale gold highlights. The braid is visually distinct from flames, with a clear open middle.
